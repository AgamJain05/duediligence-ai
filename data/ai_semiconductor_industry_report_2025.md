# AI Semiconductor Market — 2025 Annual Review

*Published by **Meridian Technology Research** — January 15, 2026*

> Data note: Company-level figures for NexaCore and QuantumEdge are Meridian's calendar-year 2025 estimates, reconciled to each company's reported fiscal-year 2025 results. Meridian is a third-party research firm; its analysis is not a company filing.

---

## Executive Summary

The AI semiconductor market reached **$142B in calendar year 2025**, up from **$68B in 2024 (+109%)**. **NexaCore Technologies** dominates with **$61.2B** in revenue, representing **43%** of the total market. The growth trajectory is expected to continue, with the market forecast at **$220–240B in 2026**.

*(Source: Meridian Technology Research, AI Semiconductor Market 2025 Annual Review, Executive Summary)*

## NexaCore Analysis

NexaCore's FY2025 results corroborate the picture of a market leader at scale: revenue of **$61.2B**, gross margin of **76%**, and net income of **$22.1B**. The **X3 ramp** is described as "unprecedented in scale." NexaCore's **SilicaFab dependence** is characterized as "the single largest concentration risk in the semiconductor industry."

Meridian notes: "NexaCore's 72% training market share and 76% gross margin suggest pricing power that may attract antitrust scrutiny." (The US Department of Justice issued a civil investigative demand to NexaCore in October 2025.)

### Financial Commentary

NexaCore's FY2025 gross margin of 76% significantly exceeds QuantumEdge's 65%, however Meridian analysis suggests NexaCore's true adjusted gross margin, excluding one-time capacity reservation fee amortization, may be closer to 71%.

### Software and Mix

NexaOS DataBridge and X3 premium pricing contributed to margin expansion from 71.9% in FY2024.

*(Source: Meridian Technology Research, NexaCore Analysis)*

## QuantumEdge Analysis

QuantumEdge reported revenue of **$28.3B (+51%)** and a gross margin of **65%**. **QuantumCloud** is growing rapidly, with **180,000 customers** and **$1.4B** in revenue.

Meridian rates QuantumEdge **"BUY"** with an FY2026 revenue target of **$39B**, within the **$38–42B** company guidance. Meridian describes the patent litigation against NexaCore as a **"binary risk"**: a favorable outcome could provide licensing leverage, while an adverse one could result in costs and distraction.

*(Source: Meridian Technology Research, QuantumEdge Analysis)*

## Geopolitical Risk — Taiwan Semiconductor Concentration

The **geographic fabrication risk** posed by Taiwan is the central structural vulnerability of the AI chip market. NexaCore's dependence on SilicaFab is "unparalleled in the semiconductor industry." QuantumEdge's use of **GlobalChip in South Korea** "reduces but does not eliminate geopolitical supply risk."

Meridian estimates that a Taiwan Strait crisis would reduce AI training capacity additions by **65–75% industrywide for 12–18 months**.

*(Source: Meridian Technology Research, Geopolitical Risk)*

---

## China Export Controls

US **EAR Part 744** restrictions effective Q2 2025 reduced combined industry China revenue by an estimated **$14.2B in CY2025**. NexaCore's China revenue fell from **$5.9B (FY2024)** to **$4.9B (FY2025)**. Further expansion of restrictions to cover **X3-class chips** is expected by mid-2026.

*(Source: Meridian Technology Research, China Export Controls)*

---

## HBM Memory Bottleneck

Both NexaCore and QuantumEdge are competing for **HBM4** from **HB Memory Corp** and **ShinKwang Electronics**. Industry HBM4 capacity is limited to approximately **2.4B units per year** against estimated demand of **4.2B units**. This bottleneck will constrain AI chip production through 2026.

*(Source: Meridian Technology Research, HBM Memory Bottleneck)*

## Outlook 2026

- Total market forecast: **$220–240B**.
- NexaCore X3 at scale plus X4 development means "no credible challenger to NexaCore's training dominance through at least 2027."
- QuantumEdge's **QE-Ultra** is the most significant competitive test; if successful, QuantumEdge could recover training share to **22–25%**.
- A rumored **IPO of SilicaFab** would change the manufacturing landscape.

*(Source: Meridian Technology Research, Outlook 2026)*
