# NexaCore Technologies, Inc. — Annual Report — Fiscal Year Ended January 26, 2025

**Ticker:** NASDAQ: NXCR | **Publication Date:** March 12, 2025 | **Document Type:** Annual Report (FY2024)

> Note: **NexaCore Technologies** uses a 52/53 week fiscal year ending on the last Sunday of January. "Fiscal Year 2024" (FY2024) ended January 26, 2025.

---

## Letter to Shareholders

*By Sarah Chen, Chief Executive Officer*

Dear Shareholders,

FY2024 was the year the AI infrastructure buildout became undeniable. Revenue grew **108% to $29.5B**, gross profit was $21.2B (gross margin 71.9%), operating income was $11.1B, and net income was $9.8B. Diluted EPS rose to $3.91 from $1.64.

In Q2 we launched the **NexaCore X2 Accelerator**. Demand exceeded even our elevated expectations: X2 delivers 2.4× the performance of X1 with 160 GB of HBM3e memory, and every hyperscale customer placed orders within weeks of launch.

Also in Q2, we completed the acquisition of **DataSync Inc**, a Seattle-based data infrastructure software company, for $2.3B in cash. DataSync's machine-learning pipeline technology strengthens our software layer and increases the value customers derive from each accelerator.

I also want to be direct about a risk. US export controls on advanced AI chips to China are tightening, and we expect our China revenue to decline materially in FY2025. We have planned accordingly, and we believe demand elsewhere more than offsets the impact.

Sarah Chen
Chief Executive Officer

*(Source: NexaCore FY2024 Annual Report, Letter to Shareholders)*

## Business Overview

### Products and Technology

#### NexaCore X2 Accelerator

Launched in **Q2 FY2024**, the X2 is our second-generation AI training accelerator.

| Specification | X1 (2022) | X2 (Q2 FY2024) |
|---------------|-----------|----------------|
| Memory | 80 GB HBM3 | 160 GB HBM3e |
| Relative performance | 1.0× | 2.4× |

#### CloudMax C1 Series

The inference-optimized **CloudMax C1 Series** continued to ramp, with strong adoption among cloud service providers.

#### NexaOS and DataBridge

**NexaOS** now incorporates DataSync's pipeline technology. DataSync's ML pipeline software has been rebranded **NexaOS DataBridge**.

*(Source: NexaCore FY2024 Annual Report, Products and Technology)*

### Acquisitions

#### DataSync Inc

NexaCore acquired **DataSync Inc** in Q2 FY2024 for **$2.3B in cash**. DataSync was a Seattle, Washington-based company with **420 employees**. The acquisition contributed **$0.4B** to FY2024 revenue and resulted in **$180M** of one-time acquisition costs. DataSync's technology is now sold as **NexaOS DataBridge**.

*(Source: NexaCore FY2024 Annual Report, Acquisitions)*

### Competition

**QuantumEdge Corp** grew 62% in its latest fiscal year, and its QE-Pro GPU is gaining share in inference workloads. However, the NexaCore X2 delivers a **2.4× performance advantage** over X1 and a significant lead in training. **AMD Instinct MI300X** is gaining traction in inference, and **Google's TPU v5** is strong inside Google's own data centers.

### Manufacturing and Supply Chain

Our **SilicaFab supply chain dependence** is unchanged: SilicaFab, based in Hsinchu, Taiwan, fabricates 100% of our X-series chips. The SilicaFab agreement was extended in FY2024 and continues through **December 2027**. X2 capacity is **constrained through H1 FY2025** while SilicaFab ramps the new node.

HBM3e memory is sole-sourced from two Korean suppliers, **HB Memory Corp** and **ShinKwang Electronics**.

*(Source: NexaCore FY2024 Annual Report, Manufacturing and Supply Chain)*

### Employees

NexaCore had **15,800 employees** at the end of FY2024, up from 12,400, including approximately 420 added through the DataSync acquisition.

## Risk Factors

### Export Control Risk (ELEVATED)

#### China Revenue Impact

China was **20% of FY2024 revenue ($5.9B)**. In **October 2024**, the US Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS) proposed new restrictions covering chips equivalent to A100-class and above. We expect China revenue to **fall materially in FY2025**. Export control risk is now our **primary risk factor**.

#### Regulatory Timeline

The proposed rules are expected to be finalized in the first half of FY2025. Licenses may be granted for limited research institutions, but commercial sales are likely to be curtailed.

*(Source: NexaCore FY2024 Annual Report, Risk Factors — Export Control Risk)*

### Manufacturing Concentration Risk

#### Taiwan Concentration

The **geographic fabrication risk** of producing 100% of X-series chips in Hsinchu, Taiwan remains our most significant operational dependency.

#### SilicaFab Capacity Ramp

The X2 ramp at SilicaFab has been challenging. Any disruption would prevent revenue recognition on our order backlog.

### Customer Concentration Risk

#### Top Customers

Our **top 3 cloud customers represented 52% of Data Center revenue in FY2024** (approximately $11.1B). These customers are large hyperscale cloud providers; we do not name them. Purchases are made under purchase orders, not long-term commitments.

### HBM Memory Supply Risk

#### Sole-Source Dependence

HBM3e is sole-sourced from **HB Memory Corp** and **ShinKwang Electronics**. Memory allocation is a production bottleneck for X2.

### Competition Risk

#### QuantumEdge in Inference

The **QE-Pro GPU** is gaining share in inference workloads, where total cost of ownership matters more than peak training performance.

### Integration Risk

#### DataSync Technology and Culture

DataSync's integration is ongoing. Technology integration, customer retention, and cultural alignment all carry execution risk.
