## Letter to Shareholders

*By Marcus Webb, Chief Executive Officer*

Dear Shareholders,

FY2025 revenue was **$28.3B (+51%)**, net income was **$4.8B**, and gross margin was **65.0%**. **QuantumCloud** exceeded expectations, generating **$1.4B in revenue** from **180,000 customers**. Marcus Webb
Chief Executive Officer

## Risk Factors

### NexaCore Competition Risk

#### X3 Performance Gap

NexaCore X3 represents a **3.1× improvement over X2**. QE-Ultra must achieve at least comparable improvement over QE-Pro to remain competitive.

#### Market Share Erosion

We risk further share erosion in the training segment.

### IP Litigation Risk

#### Case Status

In September 2025 we filed patent infringement claims against NexaCore relating to HBM memory controller architecture (**Case No. 1:2025cv08841, Northern District of California**). The outcome is uncertain, and counterclaims are possible.

### HBM Memory Allocation Risk

#### HBM4 Supply

QE-Ultra requires **HBM4**. NexaCore is competing for the same limited supply from **HB Memory Corp** and **ShinKwang Electronics**. QuantumEdge has secured **35%** of its estimated FY2026 HBM4 need.

### QuantumCloud Churn Risk

#### Monthly Churn

QuantumCloud had 180,000 customers at FY2025 year-end, but **monthly churn is 2.1%**. We must sustain growth to reach software-like margins at scale.

### Manufacturing Concentration Risk

#### GlobalChip

GlobalChip is our primary foundry. While we have no Taiwan exposure, a South Korea disruption would affect production.

*(Source: QuantumEdge FY2025 Annual Report, Risk Factors)*

## Litigation

### Patent Claims vs NexaCore

Filed **September 2025**. The claims cover **7 patents** relating to HBM memory controller and multi-chip interconnect technology. We seek injunctive relief and damages. NexaCore has denied all claims.

### Class Action Dismissed

A securities class action relating to the QE-Nano delay (Q3 FY2024) was **dismissed in December 2024**.
